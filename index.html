01 ABOUT ME

One computational
mindset.
Many biological questions.

An engineer by training.
A researcher by curiosity.

I work at the intersection of genomics, metagenomics and computational biology, connecting biological questions with thoughtful computational analysis.

My foundation in software engineering led me to a master’s degree in bioinformatics. Over approximately five years at Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), I built a substantial research background in environmental genomics, microbial research and metagenomics.

Today, I work at Hub de Médecine de Précision (HMP), a specialized precision medicine unit within the Hôpital Universitaire International Mohammed VI de Rabat, part of the Fondation Mohammed VI des Sciences et de la Santé (FM6SS) ecosystem. My work spans human whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing, germline variant calling and prioritization, and microbiome research in health. Environmental systems and human health remain complementary parts of my scientific identity, each with its own questions and purpose.

I bring programming, statistical analysis and scientific workflow development to the challenge of understanding complex biological data.
